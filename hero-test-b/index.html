The Salon

A Ladies Salon
in Clifton, Karachi

Muse by Anahita is a private salon for hair, skin, nails and makeup. We keep the atmosphere calm and the appointments unhurried, because we would rather do one thing well than rush through three.

The menu covers precision cuts and blow dries, balayage and keratin, HydraFacial and professional facials, gel and acrylic nails, threading and waxing, massage, and occasion and bridal makeup. We work with Olaplex, GK, Janssen, Thalgo, Guinot and OPI.

Our team has more than fifteen years of experience, and we're open every day from 12 to 9. Every appointment begins with a consultation, and every price is published on our menu. If we don't think a service will give you the result you want, we will tell you.
