The Journey

How Bridal Bookings Work

From your first enquiry to the wedding day.

01
6-8 Weeks Before

Consultation

Bring photographs of your outfits and jewellery, and any looks you love. We discuss your skin, your colouring and how each event should feel.

02
A Few Weeks Before

Trial

If you'd like a trial, we create the full look so you can see it in daylight and on camera, and adjust anything before the day.

03
The Final Week

Preparation

Many brides book a facial, manicure, pedicure and waxing in the last week. We advise against trying a new facial for the first time this close to the wedding.

04
Wedding Day

The Day

Arrive at your booked time with clean, moisturised skin. We complete your makeup, set your dupatta and jewellery, and see you off ready for the photographs.

Our Approach

Still You,
On Your Biggest Day.

The most common worry we hear from brides is looking heavy, or unlike themselves, in photographs. So we begin with skin preparation, match your base in natural light, and build the look around your outfit and jewellery rather than a passing trend.

The salon is private, so you can get ready without interruption. With more than fifteen years of experience behind the team, we plan the morning so that nothing is rushed.
