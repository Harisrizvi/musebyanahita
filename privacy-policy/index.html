Privacy Policy

This explains what personal information we collect when you use this website or book with us, why we collect it, and what we do with it. We keep this deliberately short and plain.

Last updated July 2026

What we collect

When you contact us or make a booking, we may collect:

  • —Your name
  • —Your phone number
  • —Your email address (if you give it)
  • —The reason for your enquiry, or the service you've booked
  • —Anything else you choose to tell us in your message

Why we collect it

Only to respond to you and to arrange and manage your appointment. We use your phone number to confirm bookings over WhatsApp, and your email to send booking confirmations.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for marketing.

Who else is involved

Some parts of this site rely on trusted third-party services, and your information passes through them:

  • —WhatsApp (Meta): how enquiries and booking messages reach us
  • —Google: enquiry details are recorded in a private Google Sheet so we can keep track of them
  • —Google Analytics: counts visits and button clicks (such as Book Now or WhatsApp) so we know which pages are useful. It doesn't tell us who you are
  • —GitHub: hosts this website

How long we keep it

We keep booking and enquiry records for as long as we need them to run the salon and maintain your client history. If you'd like your details removed, just ask.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us on +92 331 4666585 or through the enquiry form and we'll deal with it.

Cookies

This website doesn't use advertising cookies. We use Google Analytics, which sets its own cookies to count visits anonymously. You can block these with your browser settings or an ad blocker without affecting the site.

Questions

If anything here isn't clear, or you're concerned about how your information is handled, please get in touch. We'd rather you asked.
