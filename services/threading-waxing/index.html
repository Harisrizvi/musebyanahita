Threading & Waxing

Brow and face threading, Rica face waxing, and honey or Rica body waxing.

Brows and smooth skin, done carefully

Brows are shaped to your face and natural arch, taking away as little as possible. Threading gives the cleanest, most precise line. If your skin reacts to threading, Rica face wax is a gentler alternative.

For the body, choose honey wax or Rica. Honey wax is effective and economical for most skin types, while Rica is a premium formula that clients with sensitive skin often prefer. Full-body waxing is available with either.
