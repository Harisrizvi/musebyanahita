Party & Event Makeup

Soft, evening and occasion makeup, with lashes and hair styling available.

Made for the occasion

Soft makeup suits daytime events, lunches and smaller dinners: even skin, defined brows and a natural lip. Evening makeup builds more coverage and definition, and is designed to hold under artificial light and flash photography.

Bring your outfit, or a photograph of it, along with any looks you like. We work with your colouring and jewellery, and can finish with lashes and hair styling if you would like them. For nikkah, baraat and walima, please see our Bridal page.
