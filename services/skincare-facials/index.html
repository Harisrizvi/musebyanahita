HydraFacial & Facials

HydraFacial, professional facials by Janssen, Thalgo and Guinot, LED therapy and skin polishing.

Choosing the right facial

Most people book a facial by name. We would rather start with your skin. Tell your therapist what you have noticed, whether that is congestion, dullness, uneven tone or early lines, and she will recommend the treatment that addresses it, including when a simpler one will do.

The menu runs from deep cleansing and our Essential Facial to professional treatments by Janssen, Thalgo and Guinot. HydraFacial cleanses, exfoliates and hydrates in a single session with no downtime, which makes it a popular choice in the week before an event. LED light therapy can be added to any facial or booked on its own.
